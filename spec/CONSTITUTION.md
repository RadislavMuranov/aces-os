# ACES OS — CONSTITUTION

**Status:** PROPOSED CANON REVISION / E1 INPUT
**Version:** 2.0-proposed
**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Basis:** Karpathy — *Software Is Changing (Again)*, YC AI Startup School, 2025-06-17; ACES experimental record through E0.11

## Preamble

ACES OS exists to preserve human agency and psychic energy while making the Digital World continuously usable through AI agents and automation.

The Digital World is becoming an agent-native environment. LLMs are becoming a new computational substrate; agents are becoming a new class of software actor; and software is increasingly created and operated through natural-language-driven systems. The consequence for ACES is not to become another application, but to become the trusted connective and governance layer between **HUMAN, AGENTS, and DIGITAL WORLD**.

## Article I — Primary Mission

> **ACES OS is the trusted connector between HUMAN, AI AGENTS, and the DIGITAL WORLD.**

ACES does not replace the Digital World. It makes the Digital World usable without requiring the human to become its integration layer.

The primary realization target is therefore the elimination of **parasitic cross-world coordination**: context reconstruction, repeated handoffs, fragmented state, duplicated entry, unauthorized transitions, and the human maintenance of connections between otherwise capable systems.

## Article II — Human Primacy

The human remains the source of legitimate human intention and ultimate authority where a genuinely human decision is involved.

ACES exists to preserve and amplify human agency, not to silently replace it.

> **The inevitability of interaction with the Digital World is not a justification for expending human psychic energy.**

Operational principle:

> **ACES removes parasitic friction, not necessary friction.**

## Article III — Three-Sided Connector

ACES must be designed for three simultaneous participants:

`HUMAN ↔ ACES ↔ AGENTS ↔ DIGITAL WORLD`

The connector must support:

1. human-facing comprehension and control;
2. agent-facing machine-readable context, capabilities, and contracts;
3. digital-world execution through existing owner systems.

ACES must not assume that human GUI, conventional API, and agent interface are equivalent surfaces.

## Article IV — Owner Boundaries

Existing systems remain owners of their domain artifacts and actions unless evidence proves otherwise.

ACES coordinates; it does not unnecessarily become the owner of:

- communication;
- time;
- documents;
- thinking;
- code;
- project execution;
- relationships;
- accounting or financial records;
- specialized domain state.

No new workspace is justified merely by connectivity.

## Article V — Model Independence

No LLM provider, model, agent framework, GitHub account, MCP server, or external platform is an ACES trust boundary or source of architectural authority.

LLMs are computational substrates and probabilistic capabilities, not authorities.

`Capability ≠ Reliability ≠ Authority`

Model replacement must not destroy ACES continuity, provenance, memory, policy, or authority state.

## Article VI — Governed Cognitive Action

The fundamental ACES execution unit is not a prompt alone. It is a **Governed Cognitive Action**:

`Intent → Context → Evidence/Knowledge → Decision → Authority → Execution → Verification → AcceptedState`

Every autonomous transition must have an explicit authority boundary appropriate to its risk and scope.

Technical permission is not decision authority.

Inference is not evidence.

Generation is not acceptance.

## Article VII — Verification Before Trust

Because agent intelligence is jagged and probabilistic, ACES must never infer trust from general model capability alone.

Trust must be task-, context-, evidence-, and risk-sensitive.

The canonical control loop is:

`GENERATE → VERIFY → ACCEPT / REJECT → LEARN`

Verification must be cheap enough and tight enough to support bounded autonomy.

## Article VIII — Bounded Autonomy

Autonomy is a controlled dimension, not a binary property.

ACES must support an explicit autonomy continuum:

`OBSERVE → RECOMMEND → PREPARE → APPROVE → BOUNDED AUTONOMY → DELEGATED AUTONOMY`

Each level must bind to capability, scope, policy, authority, provenance, audit, revocation, and expiration as appropriate.

Autonomy must be earned by demonstrated reliability and verification, not declared by the agent.

## Article IX — Memory and Learning

LLMs are not assumed to possess durable personal continuity.

ACES therefore distinguishes:

- Context — current working state;
- Memory — retained experience;
- Knowledge — consolidated understanding;
- Evidence — support for claims;
- Policy — governing rule;
- Skill — executable capability;
- World Model — represented state of reality;
- Strategy — learned method for future behavior.

Learned strategies are initially candidates. They do not become canonical merely because an agent generated them.

`Experience → Reflection → Candidate Strategy → Evidence → Evaluation → Approved Strategy`

## Article X — Provenance and Uncertainty

ACES must preserve the distinction between:

`observed / inferred / proposed / verified / accepted`

A missing artifact is not a found artifact.

An ambiguous request is not a task.

A probable relationship is not an established relationship.

A generated answer is not evidence merely because it is coherent.

## Article XI — Accepted State

A technical action is not complete until the resulting state is verified and accepted according to the applicable contract.

ACES treats **AcceptedState** as the boundary between attempted execution and trusted continuity.

Small, reversible, verifiable state transitions are preferred over large opaque autonomous changes.

## Article XII — Agent-Native Digital World

ACES must actively support the emergence of agent-native interfaces.

External systems should expose machine-readable:

- capabilities;
- context;
- schemas;
- permissions;
- provenance;
- state transitions;
- verification signals.

MCP and similar protocols are transport/integration mechanisms. They do not automatically provide ACES-level authority, provenance, or epistemic guarantees.

## Article XIII — Reality Layer

The human-facing Reality Layer exposes only the minimum cross-world state required for effective action.

Canonical output remains:

`NOW / IMPORTANT / NEXT`

Internal orchestration must remain invisible unless inspection is required.

## Article XIV — Evidence Before Architecture

The new mission is strategically primary, but implementation remains experimentally governed.

No architecture is admitted merely because it is elegant or aligned with the future.

The connector hypothesis must survive:

1. strongest existing baseline;
2. minimality test;
3. automation/commodity test;
4. owner-boundary test;
5. agency test;
6. provenance/uncertainty test;
7. measurable human-integration-work reduction.

Strategic priority does not suspend falsifiability.

## Article XV — Global Objective

The long-term objective is not to build another digital environment.

It is to make the existing and emerging Digital World behave as a coherent, agent-accessible environment for a human while preserving human agency, ownership boundaries, evidence, provenance, security, memory, and recoverability.

## Constitutional Formula

`ACES OS = HUMAN AGENCY + AGENT CAPABILITY + DIGITAL-WORLD CONNECTIVITY + EPISTEMIC/SECURITY GOVERNANCE`

with the non-negotiable condition:

`Human psychic energy must not be spent maintaining connections that machines can maintain safely.`
