## Decision vocabulary

- `REJECT`: reproducible conflict with an ACES requirement or no demonstrated benefit.
- `RECOMMEND_FOR_REVIEW`: evidence supports a separate governance decision; not automatic acceptance.
- `ACCEPTED`: prohibited within this research stage. Requires a separate explicit approval process.
