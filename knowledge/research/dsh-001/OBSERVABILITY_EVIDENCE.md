# Observability vs Evidence

DSH records: SessionEvent stream, assistant/chunks, tool call/result, telemetry, titles.  

| Term | DSH fit |
|------|--------|
| Log / trace | Strong |
| Session history | Strong |
| Evidence (ACES governance) | Partial — not Decision/AcceptedState |
| Provenance (authz) | Weak relative to ASA AuthorizationEvent |
| Proof | Not claimed |

Do not conflate session log with Canonical evidence.
