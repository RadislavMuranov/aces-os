---

## 1. Purpose

Reconstruct **historical decisions and their provenance** from the two verified source corpora:

- CHATGPT-CORPUS-001
- DEEPSEEK-CORPUS-001

This step produces evidence-backed reconstructions of decisions, principles, and control patterns that actually appeared in the corpora. It does **not** invent missing documents.

---

## 2. Hard Scope Boundaries

**In scope**
- Identification of historical decisions, principles, and control patterns present in the corpora.
- Provenance map: corpus → conversation ID → evidence location → confidence.
- Distinction between observed wording and later synthesis.
- Classification of each reconstructed item as:
  - OBSERVED PRINCIPLE
  - DISTRIBUTED PRECURSOR
  - PARTIAL ARTIFACT
  - NOT RECOVERABLE AS EXACT ARTIFACT

**Out of scope (forbidden in this branch)**
- Construction / implementation of Evolution Kernel
- Promotion of Evolution Program to Canonical
- Modification of any protected canonical artifact
- Creation of new code, skills, hooks, or runtime controls
- Mixing other evolutionary decisions (E0.4, E0.5, etc.)
- Silent second evolution layer
