| Class | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| EXACT HISTORICAL ARTIFACT | Recoverable named historical file/document as such |

| LATER SYNTHESIS | Formulated after the corpus window (or only in current session) |
| UNRESOLVED | Insufficient evidence to classify |
| NOT RECOVERED | Exact artifact sought but not found |

Distributed precursor evidence is **never** promoted to EXACT HISTORICAL ARTIFACT in this report.
