---

## 3. Binding constraints

1. ACES OS does not evolve for the sake of new abstraction, novelty, completeness, or architectural expansion.
2. Every evolution branch must have a concrete observed basis: failure, unmet requirement, or measurable deficiency.
3. The burden of proof lies on the proposed change.
4. The hypothesis must be testable and falsifiable.
5. Minimal intervention is preferred.
6. Promotion requires measurable improvement.
7. Absent improvement, the change is rejected or removed.
8. Where applicable, ablation is mandatory.
9. Recursive production of new abstractions without evidence is forbidden.
10. **STOP is a valid and complete outcome** of an evolution process.

---

## 4. Counterfactual Test (mandatory before any new evolution branch)

> **What concretely will continue to break or remain unresolved if we add nothing?**

If there is no material observed failure / unmet requirement / measurable deficiency:

```
BRANCH = BLOCKED
```
