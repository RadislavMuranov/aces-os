## 4. Architecture

```text
SourceContribution[] + task
        → ContextCompiler.compile()
        → ContextPackage + CompilationEvidence
```

- Pure / deterministic item IDs from `(owner_system, source_object_id, claim_key)`.
- Permissions = union of **source** permissions only.
- Conflicts detected by shared `claim_key` with differing `observed_state`; both observations retained; `resolved=False`.
- `INFERRED` / `UNKNOWN` never promoted to `OBSERVED`.
- Evidence record is instrumentation only; `verification_result` and `accepted_state_reference` remain unset by the compiler.
