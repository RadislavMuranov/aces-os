# DECISION — KARPATHY / P0 CONNECTOR PRIORITY

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Branch:** `evolution/karpathy-agent-native-priority`
**Status:** Strategic decision recorded; Canon promotion pending controlled reconciliation

## Decision

Make the **Human–Agent–Digital World Connector** the highest-priority ACES OS concept for research and realization.

## Why

The 2025-06-17 Andrej Karpathy YC AI Startup School talk identifies a converging transition:

`Software 3.0 → LLM as computational substrate → agents → partial autonomy → verification → agent-native Digital World`.

The ACES architectural question is therefore sharpened from:

> Can a new ACES workspace coordinate the Digital World?

to:

> **Can a trusted connective layer let a human use an increasingly agent-native Digital World as one coherent environment without personally maintaining its connections?**

## What changes

1. Connector mission becomes P0.
2. Context continuity becomes P0 dependency.
3. Capability/reliability separation becomes P0.
4. Authority/autonomy contracts become P0.
5. Governed Cognitive Action becomes P0 runtime target.
6. Verification and AcceptedState remain mandatory trust boundaries.
7. Agent-native contracts become first-class integration targets.
8. Memory/learning are treated as continuity infrastructure, not isolated features.
9. Reality Layer remains the human-facing surface, not a new workspace.

## What does not change

- Evidence precedes architecture.
- Existing owner systems remain owners.
- No new workspace merely for connectivity.
- Human agency remains authoritative where genuinely human judgment is required.
- Uncertainty and provenance must be preserved.
- External platforms and LLMs are not ACES trust boundaries.
- Strategic priority does not imply automatic Canon promotion.

## Acceptance test

The P0 thesis succeeds only if a controlled baseline/treatment experiment demonstrates a material reduction in human integration work while preserving owner boundaries, authority, provenance, uncertainty, security, and recoverability.

## Canon status

This decision is an **E1 input**. It must be reconciled with the existing Canon and evolution controls before being promoted to immutable Canon.
