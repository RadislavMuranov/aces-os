# ACES OS — Pre-KMA-12 Recovery Roadmap

**Status:** ACTIVE / RECOVERY CONTROL ROADMAP  
**Scope:** Repository and project-state recovery before KMA-12  
**Authority:** Forensic/recovery planning artifact. It does not establish architecture, product value, ACES necessity, or Canonical status.  
**KMA-11:** FROZEN / MATERIALIZED / RE-VERIFIED  
**KMA-12:** FROZEN until the exit condition below is satisfied  
**KMA-13:** NOT AUTHORIZED

## 1. Purpose

This roadmap defines the pre-KMA-12 recovery work required to reconstruct the actual project picture without creating a new epistemic layer, erasing historical research, or performing premature repository cleanup.

The immediate objective is **containment and reconstruction, not repair**.

## 2. Non-Negotiable Principles

1. Preserve repository history and research before cleanup.
2. Do not treat repository presence, commit messages, branch names, specifications, or governance artifacts as proof of the claims they contain.
3. Do not collapse research into architecture.
4. Do not delete historical research merely because an architectural hypothesis is rejected or removed.
5. Do not promote historical, hypothesized, reported, or ChatGPT-derived claims without sufficient independent support.
6. No mass deletion, rename, consolidation, Canon rewrite, architecture rewrite, or new governance layer during this recovery sequence unless separately and explicitly authorized.
7. Unknown remains UNKNOWN; insufficient evidence results in STOP ACQUISITION rather than a forced conclusion.
8. Every recovery result remains a forensic/recovery result until independently verified and explicitly promoted.

## 4. Stage Definitions

### R0 — CONTAINMENT

Freeze architectural expansion, repository cleanup, Canon changes, governance expansion, and repair. Preserve history. Establish the recovery boundary and prevent further epistemic contamination.

**Exit:** repository may be inspected without changing the object under investigation.

### R1 — REPOSITORY REALITY MAP

Inventory the actual repository surface: main, branches, commits, files, research, specifications, governance, experiments, implementation, tests, historical artifacts, and materialized evidence.

**Required distinction:** existence/location is an observation of repository state, not proof of claim truth.

### R2 — BRANCH / COMMIT ARCHAEOLOGY

Reconstruct temporal and materialization history: origins, commits, branch transitions, merges, deletions/renames where recoverable, and high-impact lineage chains.

**Output:** repository-materialization lineage, explicitly separated from conversational/source lineage.

### R3 — CLAIM DEPENDENCY GRAPH

Map material claims and decisions to their upstream sources, transformations, artifacts, and downstream dependents.

**Question:** if an upstream premise fails, which later claims/artifacts lose support?

### R4 — RESEARCH RECOVERY

Recover primary research artifacts, experiments, negative results, rejected hypotheses, contradictions, scope restrictions, execution status, and provenance behind compressed summaries and indexes.

**Rule:** research remains preserved even where the architecture it investigated does not survive.

### R5 — EXECUTION / VERIFICATION AUDIT

Audit claims such as PASS, COMPLETE, VERIFIED, EXECUTED, VALIDATED, IMPLEMENTED, PROVEN, ACCEPTED, and CANONICAL against executable traces, raw outputs, tests, measurements, or independent verification.

**Default when evidence is insufficient:** CLAIMED / NOT VERIFIED or UNKNOWN.

### R6 — EPISTEMIC BLAST-RADIUS ANALYSIS

For each high-impact premise, determine which conclusions, specifications, governance rules, architecture proposals, and downstream artifacts depend on it.

**Goal:** identify propagation of unsupported or weakly supported claims without declaring dependent artifacts false automatically.

### R7 — ARCHITECTURE DEPENDENCY ANALYSIS

Separate demonstrated product requirements from architecture-derived requirements. Identify components whose necessity depends primarily on other ACES abstractions, historical assumptions, or unverified research conclusions.

**No architectural redesign is authorized by this stage.**

### R8 — DOCUMENTATION / IMPLEMENTATION GAP

Measure the relationship between documentation, governance, specifications, research, code, tests, executed scenarios, and measured external outcomes.

**Goal:** establish actual maturity asymmetry rather than infer quality from document volume.

### R9 — EXTERNAL SUBSTITUTION AUDIT

For each candidate ACES mechanism, test whether the relevant real-world task can be solved by existing native functionality, connectors, generic automation, or a minimal coordinator before considering ACES-specific mechanisms.

**Evidence must be task-level and, where applicable, executable.**

### R10 — SIX-MONTH COUNTERFACTUAL

Compare the actual six-month trajectory with a minimal evidence-first trajectory to identify which investments produced recoverable evidence/value and which were primarily process or architectural sedimentation.

**This is not a sunk-cost judgment and must not erase useful historical work.**

### R11 — MINIMAL PRODUCT RECONSTRUCTION

Reconstruct the smallest credible real-world product problem, baseline, intervention, measurable outcome, and residual gap that survive the preceding forensic work.

**No architecture is assumed.**

## 5. KMA-12 Entry Gate

KMA-12 MUST REMAIN FROZEN until all of the following are satisfied:

- the repository surface has been fully inventoried to the declared scope;
- branch/commit archaeology has been completed to the declared scope;
- high-impact transformation and claim dependencies are reconstructed;
- relevant primary research and negative evidence have been recovered or explicitly marked unrecoverable;
- execution/verification claims have been audited;
- epistemic blast radius has been assessed for material high-impact premises;
- architecture dependencies have been separated from evidence-bearing facts;
- documentation/implementation/product-value asymmetry has been established from repository evidence;
- external substitution has been assessed where relevant;
- the six-month counterfactual has been completed;
- the minimal product problem and its surviving evidence state have been reconstructed;
- unresolved areas are explicitly listed rather than silently promoted;
- the resulting recovery picture has been independently re-verified against the repository and primary evidence available within the declared scope.

**KMA-12 entry does not mean KMA-12 will PASS.** It means only that the forensic reconstruction is sufficiently complete to make a materialization verdict without relying on an incomplete picture.

## 6. Repository Safety Rules

During R0–R11:

- no architectural repair;
- no implementation expansion;
- no Canon promotion;
- no new ACES subsystem;
- no new object model;
- no new persistent state;
- no mass cleanup;
- no deletion of research/evidence merely for simplification;
- no new governance layer created to compensate for recovery findings.

Any exception requires an explicit, separately recorded authorization.
